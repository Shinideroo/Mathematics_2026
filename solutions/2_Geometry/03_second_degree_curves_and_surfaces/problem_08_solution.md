### Exercise 8. Sphere
Reduce

$$
x^2+y^2+z^2-4x+2y-6z-2=0
$$

to canonical form. Find the center and radius. Give two points on the sphere and check them in the equation.

> **Why this exercise:** transfers the technique known from circles from two dimensions to three.

## Solution

Complete all three squares:

$$
(x-2)^2-4+(y+1)^2-1+(z-3)^2-9-2=0.
$$

Thus the canonical form is

$$
(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2+(z-3)^2=16.
$$

The sphere has center $(2,-1,3)$ and radius $4$.

Choose $(6,-1,3)$ and $(-2,-1,3)$, each four units horizontally from the center. Their squared distances from the center are $16$. The original left sides are

$$
36+1+9-24-2-18-2=0,
$$

$$
4+1+9+8-2-18-2=0,
$$

respectively, verifying both points in the original equation.
