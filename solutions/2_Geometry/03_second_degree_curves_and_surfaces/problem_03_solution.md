### Exercise 3. Ellipse
For

$$
\frac{x^2}{25}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1,
$$

find the semiaxis lengths, the intercepts with the coordinate axes, and make a sketch. Explain along which axis the ellipse is more stretched and how this is visible in the equation.

> **Why this exercise:** teaches how to read the size and orientation of an ellipse without additional conceptual machinery.

## Solution

Compare with $x^2/a^2+y^2/b^2=1$. The semiaxes are $a=5$ horizontally and $b=3$ vertically. The center is $(0,0)$.

Setting $y=0$ gives $x=\pm5$; setting $x=0$ gives $y=\pm3$. Thus the intercepts are $(\pm5,0)$ and $(0,\pm3)$.

~~~text
                 y
                 3
           .-----+-----.
        .-'      |      '-.
  (-5,0)---------+---------(5,0)  x
        '-.      |      .-'
           '-----+-----'
                -3
~~~

The larger denominator $25$ permits $|x|$ up to $5$, compared with $|y|$ up to $3$, so the ellipse is stretched along the horizontal axis. Substituting the intercepts gives $25/25=1$ or $9/9=1$, checking both scale and orientation.
