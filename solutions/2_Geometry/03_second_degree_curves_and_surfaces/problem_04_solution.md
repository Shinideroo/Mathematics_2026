### Exercise 4. Parabola
For

$$
y^2=12x,
$$

identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction. Find points on the parabola for $x=3$ and use them to make a sketch.

> **Why this exercise:** teaches how to move from the equation of a parabola to its basic geometry.

## Solution

The equation has the form $y^2=4px$, with $p=3$. Its vertex is $(0,0)$, symmetry axis is the $x$-axis, and it opens toward positive $x$, because $x=y^2/12\ge0$.

For $x=3$, $y^2=36$, so the two points are $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$.

~~~text
y
^               * (3,6)
|          .-'
|      .-'
|   .-'
O--<--------------------------> x
|   '-.
|      '-.
|          '-.
|               * (3,-6)
~~~

The vertex is $O$; the two arms lie to its right. Substitution checks $6^2=(-6)^2=12\cdot3=36$. The occurrence of $y^2$ ensures symmetry under $y\mapsto-y$.
